My exact location on vast map of methodological approaches is best described as 'off course, but somehow still exactly where I should be'. 

I creatively mix computational methods with qualitative interrogation. For every project I design data collection, data storage and analysis pipelines that make sense for the project itself, stringing together different techniques and tools that get us to the answer.

My qualitative background means I always prioritise human interpretation, and take a critical approach to what exactly the techniques are doing and what the output means.

Some of the tools and techniques I've worked with include:

- SQL, NOSQL and Graph Databases
